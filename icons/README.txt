This folder is for social media and contact icons for your website footer.

Add the following icons (PNG or SVG recommended):
- facebook.png (Facebook icon)
- phone.png (Phone icon)
- gmail.png (Gmail icon)

You can download free icons from sites like https://icons8.com or https://fontawesome.com/icons and place them here.

Example usage in your HTML:
<img src="icons/facebook.png" alt="Facebook" style="width:28px;height:28px;">
<img src="icons/phone.png" alt="Phone" style="width:28px;height:28px;">
<img src="icons/gmail.png" alt="Gmail" style="width:28px;height:28px;">
